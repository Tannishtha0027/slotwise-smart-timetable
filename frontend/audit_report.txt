REQUIRED UPLOAD FORMAT:
- **Format:** Multi-sheet Excel workbook (`.xlsx`)
- **Reasoning:** A single CSV file cannot cleanly represent the five distinct, highly-relational entities (TimeSlots, Rooms, Faculty, Students, Exams) required by the `CollegeData` schema without massive denormalization, redundancy, and risk of data anomalies. A single Excel workbook with dedicated sheets is the simplest, most user-friendly approach for a single-file upload.
- **Required Sheets & Columns:**
  1. `TimeSlots`: id, date (YYYY-MM-DD), start_time (HH:MM), end_time (HH:MM), duration_minutes
  2. `Rooms`: id, capacity, building, available_slots (comma-separated TimeSlot IDs)
  3. `Faculty`: id, name, max_invigilations_per_day, available_slots (comma-separated TimeSlot IDs)
  4. `Exams`: id, course_name, duration_minutes, required_invigilators
  5. `Students`: id, batch, enrolled_exams (comma-separated Exam IDs)
  *(Note: `enrolled_students` on the Exam model can be reverse-engineered dynamically by the parser based on the Students' `enrolled_exams` column to prevent duplicate entry).*
